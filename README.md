1-)Which site has the largest successful launches?

Answer: KSC LC-39A has the largest number of successful launches, accounting for 41.7% (10 successful landings) of all successful launches.

2-)Which site has the highest launch success rate?

Answer: KSC LC-39A has the highest launch success rate at 76.9% (10 successful launches out of 13 attempts).

3-)Which payload range(s) has the highest launch success rate?

Answer: The 2,000 kg – 4,000 kg payload range (particularly 3,000 kg – 4,000 kg) has the highest launch success rate.

4-)Which payload range(s) has the lowest launch success rate?

Answer: The 6,000 kg – 9,000 kg payload range has the lowest launch success rate (0%, as there are no successful landings above 6,000 kg in this dataset).

5-)Which F9 Booster version (v1.0, v1.1, FT, B4, B5, etc.) has the highest launch success rate?

Answer: B5 has the highest overall success rate at 100% (1/1 launch), while FT (Full Thrust) has the highest success rate among multi-launch booster versions at 66.7% (16/24 launches).
